# Electromangnetics_project
Field Grading Material (FGM) is a technique used to control the electric field stress in AC and DC under high voltage
applications. FGM is performed by spatial distribution of electric conductivity within the HVDC-GIL spacer to reduce the electric
field stress at the junction of SF6 gas, spacer and conductor/enclosure and is investigated by DC-steady state in stationary simulations.
However, in this work, also DC-on process is investigated. Under DC-on process condition, spatial distribution of electric conductivity
model is not sufficient to control the electric field stress, since also transient electric field occurs during the process. Hence,
combination of electric conductivity and permittivity spatial distribution models has to be applied to obtain a reduced electric field
stress around the spacer, which is investigated by transient simulations.
